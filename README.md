## Who We Are

Wajhat Al Sawt is a global provider of business process outsourcing and contact center services, helping
enterprises reduce costs, streamline operations and accelerate growth.

Founded in 2016, Wajhat Al Sawt has grown from a specialist outsourcing firm into a global BPO provider serving
more than 500 enterprises in over 50 countries. We combine cutting-edge technology with world-class talent to
deliver solutions that drive measurable business impact for our clients.

Today, our teams operate around the clock across multiple time zones, giving clients the flexibility to scale
support, operations and growth without the cost of building these capabilities in-house.

## Our Services

**Call Center Outsourcing.** Dedicated contact center teams configured to your requirements, whether onshore,
nearshore, offshore or fully virtual, including customer experience management, Contact Center as a Service and
workforce optimization.

**BPO & Business Support Services.** Back office operations and support, finance and accounting, HR and
recruitment, revenue cycle management, workforce management, management support, and enterprise and small
business contact centers.

**After-Hours & Overflow Support.** Dedicated SMS, live chat and email teams that keep your customers supported
outside business hours and during periods of peak demand.

**Order Taking & Billing Services.** Order taking, billing and payment support, and continuity subscription
management.

**Inbound & Outbound Support.** Inbound contact center outsourcing, omnichannel support, lead qualification,
cross-selling and upselling, appointment setting, and sales and lead generation.

**Customer Service & Support.** Customer support, technical support, call answering and virtual reception, help
desk services and content moderation.

**AI & Technology Services.** AI data annotation, AI as a Service, AI implementation and human-in-the-loop
operations.
